01 / BALANCE

KUZUSHI

Kuzushi means off-balancing. A partner is harder to move when their centre of mass stays over their base of support. Movement, grips, and timing can change that relationship.

COM → EDGE OF BASE
02 / LEVERAGE

TORQUE

A force can rotate an object more effectively when it acts farther from a pivot. In a simplified model, torque magnitude is τ = F × r, where r is the perpendicular lever arm.

TORQUE = FORCE × LEVER ARM
03 / MOVEMENT

TAI-SABAKI

Body movement and turning help you reach a useful position while maintaining your own balance. Foot placement, posture, and timing matter more than pulling with the arms alone.

POSTURE + POSITION + TIMING
